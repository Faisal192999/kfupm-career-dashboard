Method & evidence

What is measured—and what is modeled

Scoring method

Each major receives four pillar scores. The dashboard multiplies each score by your normalized priority weight, then sums the results.

Overall = Σ (pillar score × normalized weight)

Evidence: official Saudi sector size, jobs, investment, career-family maps, KFUPM curriculum breadth, and recognized workforce/salary research.

Model: promotion timing, relative lifestyle, title portability, and the final 0–10 scores. No claim is made that LinkedIn provided a longitudinal KFUPM alumni dataset.

Compensation: assessed as relative growth and ceiling, not precise fresh-graduate salary. Packages vary materially by employer tier, location, allowances, performance, and role scarcity.

Core sources

Primary Saudi sources are prioritized. Secondary workforce and compensation studies are used only where official major-level data do not exist.
